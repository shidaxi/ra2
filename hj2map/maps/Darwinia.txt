Darwinia
2-8 Players
Approx. $570,000 on map
Best Played 3v3, East vs. West
Lots of eye candy!!

Description: This map is set in 65,000,000 B.C.  Einstein tried a little experiment with his Time Machine, and sent at large expidetion team sixty-five million years into the past.  Unforunately, the main expidetion team was directed into a Tyrannosaur nest!  Most of them got out in time, but some were trapped in the woods, and due to hunger and paranoia, they will shoot anything that moves.  The rest of the team set up a foreward base to the southeast, and an airstip in some badlands in the north.  Your team is here to keep the others from acquiring the time machine, but look our for hordes of monkeys, they will kill your infantry without thinking twice.
